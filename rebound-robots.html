How to play

  • Each round flips one target. Get the robot of that color onto it. The swirling vortex target accepts any robot.
  • A robot moves in a straight line and keeps sliding until it hits a wall or another robot. It cannot stop partway.
  • Every robot counts toward your move total, so use the others as blockers.
  • Robots stay where they land, so each round starts from the last round's finish.
  • Solo: solve each target and compare with the fewest moves possible, found by a built-in solver.
  • Party: pass-and-play for a group around one screen. Robots are locked while everyone studies the board. The first bid starts the sand timer, and anyone may bid until it runs out. The lowest bid demonstrates first (ties go to whoever bid first). Reach the target within your bid to win the chip. Fail and the next lowest bidder tries.
  • Keys: 1–4 or R G B Y pick a robot, arrows move it, Z undoes, Esc resets the round, N is next target.
  • All 17 targets make a full game. Each board is randomly built the way the real one is: an L-shaped wall at every target and two edge walls per quarter.
  • Your game in progress and every finished Solo game are saved in this browser, so you can close the page and pick up later. They stay on this device only.
