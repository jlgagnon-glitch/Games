How to play

Bea and Dee move together, one step at a time. Up and down work the same for both. Left and right are mirrored: press ← and Bea steps left while Dee steps right.

If a twin walks into a wall, that twin stays put while the other one moves. That's how you get them out of step.

Win when both twins stand on their own goals at the same moment. Try to match the par, the fewest moves possible.
