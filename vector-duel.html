How to play
  1. The sector is 200 cm × 100 cm with the origin at the center. Angles are measured in degrees counter-clockwise from the +x axis.
  2. Each turn both teams secretly choose a thrust vector: 0, 10, 20 or 30 cm in any direction.
  3. There is no friction in space. Your thrust adds to your velocity, and your velocity is how far you move this turn:
    vnew = vold + T   rnew = rold + vnew
    So each move is the sum of every thrust you have fired.
  4. After moving, you may launch a missile (5 per game). It keeps your ship's velocity plus 40 cm/turn in the direction you aim: vm = vship + 40∠φ. Missiles fly straight for 4 turns, moving at the same time as the ships.
  5. A ship is destroyed if any missile — including its own — passes within 6 cm of its center during a move. Ships that pass within 10 cm of each other collide and both are destroyed.
  6. Fly off the board and you are lost in space.
  7. Win by destroying the enemy or watching them drift away. If both ships are lost on the same turn, or the turn limit runs out, the game is a draw.

The ring around the active ship is your protractor. Faint dots show each ship's past positions; the gap between the last two dots is its current velocity.

Game settings

Training aids draw your velocity, thrust, and resultant head-to-tail before you commit, plus your missile's path. Turn them off when students should do the math themselves. Training aids can be switched on or off mid-game.
