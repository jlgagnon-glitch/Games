How to play
  • Take turns placing a button on an empty point. Red goes first. Blue places two on its first turn, then everyone places one.
  • Five in a row wins, along any of the three directions.
  • Turn them: trap exactly two enemy buttons in a straight line between your new button and another of yours, and both switch to your color. They count for you right away.
  • Sewn down: a turned button gets stitched in place and can never be turned again.
