    House rules
    • Six cards each, 53-card deck with one joker. You play against three computers, every player for themselves.
    • Bidding goes once around, starting left of the dealer. Minimum bid is 3 (because of the draw), maximum 6. The dealer may take the high bid at the same number. If everyone passes, the dealer is stuck at 3.
    • The bidder names trump. Then everyone, starting left of the dealer, may throw out any non-trump cards and draw back to six.
    • The bidder leads first. You must follow trump when trump is led. On a non-trump lead you must follow suit or trump; you may only throw off when you have neither.
    • The joker is a trump that ranks below the 2.
    • High 1, Low 1, Jack of trump 1, Joker 2, Game 1. Six points in all. High and low go to the player who played them (see settings for low). Jack and joker go to whoever takes the trick.
    • Game counts captured cards: ten 10, ace 4, king 3, queen 2, jack 1. A tie for most game gives no point.
    • Make your bid and you score everything you took. Miss it and you lose the bid. Everyone else scores what they took. If the bidder reaches the target, the bidder goes out first.
