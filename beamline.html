Show the solution?

Your parts will be replaced with the answer. The level won't count as solved, but you can clear the board and solve it again.

How to play

Each laser fires a straight beam. Use the parts in your tray to send light to every crystal.

Win when every crystal glows in exactly its own color and no beam touches a bomb. Light mixes: red + green = yellow, red + blue = magenta, green + blue = cyan, and all three make white.
